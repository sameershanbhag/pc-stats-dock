The panel as a display

Keep it for the dock only

When the panel is plugged in, macOS may make it the main display and move your windows onto it. With this on, the agent puts your big monitor back as main, parks the panel where you say, and moves stray windows back, every time.

Right side of the panel

Buttons, message feeds, or both

“AI chats” lists every Claude Code, Codex or Cursor turn that finished or is waiting for you; tapping one on the panel jumps to that terminal tab, editor window or app. Install the hooks once with the button in that feed. Slack needs a token from a small app you create at api.slack.com (2 minutes, details in the README). Teams via Microsoft needs an app registration in your tenant and a one-time sign-in. “Notification Center” needs no setup at all: it shows what macOS just notified you about for the chosen apps, after you give “PC Stats Panel” Full Disk Access.
