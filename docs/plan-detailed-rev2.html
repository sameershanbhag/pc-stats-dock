Hardware project · build plan · revision 2

A 10.1-inch wide touch panel that docks on the desk, snaps into the case, travels to the office, and can run wireless

A real second monitor (HDMI or one USB-C cable, no drivers) in a 3D-printed dock with a 12-key macro pad and a knob. The panel's own USB hub replaces the dock electronics from revision 1, and a Raspberry Pi 5 hidden in the base makes it wireless.

Decision

Magedok T101F. The wide shape and the built-in hub both simplify the build.

  • Only 132 mm tall. It stands on a PSU shroud without covering the graphics card. The 16:10 10.1″ was 165 mm and reached into the card.
  • 249 mm wide is a natural sensor-panel shape: gauges left, touch grid right, or one long strip.
  • Three USB-A ports on the monitor act as a hub for whatever is on its USB-C. The macro pad plugs into the monitor. One cable from a laptop carries video, touch, power and the macro pad. That is the whole dock, electrically.
  • Two USB-C ports: one full-function, one power-only, so the charger powers the panel while the PC or Pi only carries data.
RejectedWhy
Raspberry Pi as the displayA Pi cannot be a monitor. It can be the wireless brain behind a real monitor, which is what revision 2 does.
Elgato Stream DeckNeeds Elgato's software on the host. Useless on a locked office PC.

Going wireless

Three ways, in the order I'd recommend them

What wireless does and doesn't remove

It removes the cable to the PC. It does not remove power: the panel draws about 5 W and the Pi 5 another 3–6 W. On a desk that is one charger cable. For a genuinely cordless panel, a 20 000 mAh USB-C PD power bank runs panel + Pi for roughly five hours, the panel alone for ten or more.

A · Pi 5 thin clientThe dashboard is a web page served by the agent on your PC. A Pi 5 in the dock base runs Chromium full-screen at 1540×720 over Wi-Fi. Taps post actions to the agent, which presses the key or switches the desktop. A small service on the Pi forwards the macro pad's keys the same way. Software

Agent, dashboard, firmware, Pi

The PC side is unchanged from revision 1: LibreHardwareMonitor publishes sensors at /data.json, a Node or Python agent polls it every 500 ms, serves the dashboard on port 4400 over a WebSocket and runs actions on POST /action/<id>. An Edge kiosk window is pinned to the 1540×720 display by size. Tablet PC Settings → Setup maps touch to the right screen. Layouts change to fit 1540×720: a left 60 % gauge strip with a right 3×4 touch grid, or one full-width strip for the case.

New for the Pi

software/pi/
  install.sh          Pi OS Lite + Wayland kiosk (cage or labwc) + Chromium
  kiosk.service       waits for http://gaming-pc:4400/health, then launches Chromium
  macropad-forward.py evdev: grabs the MacroPad, maps F13–F24 to POST /action/<id>
  moonlight.md        Sunshine + Virtual Display Driver on the PC, Moonlight-Qt pairing
chromium --kiosk --noerrdialogs --disable-infobars --ozone-platform=wayland \
  --window-size=1540,720 "http://gaming-pc.local:4400/?layout=desk"

Wireless-monitor mode: install the Virtual Display Driver on Windows, set one virtual monitor to 1540×720 at 60 Hz in its settings file, add an app in Sunshine whose output is that display. Moonlight on the Pi pairs once with a PIN and then auto-connects.

Before you buy

Fifteen minutes that save a return

Office laptop
USB-C with video (⚡ or DisplayPort logo)? Otherwise its HDMI plus a USB-A works the same way.
IT policy
External displays and USB keyboards allowed? USB storage is often blocked, which is why the MacroPad's boot profile hides its drive.
Gaming PC
Free GPU HDMI? Else the DP→HDMI adapter. Motherboard USB-C with DP Alt Mode? Then one cable at home.
Case
Flat spot at least 260 × 140 mm with 25 mm clearance (the panel is 20 mm thick). Free PCIe slot cover, spare SATA connector.
Wi-Fi
5 GHz reachable at the desk for the wireless mode. Ethernet to the dock is even better.
Printer
Your own, a makerspace, or JLC3DP.

Known traps

Gotchas

  • Stock. Magedok's store is sold out. Amazon and Newegg carry the same SKU; the price drifts between $99 and $129.
  • Power port voltage is unconfirmed. Keep the included adapter as the dock brick if the Anker 30 W does not light it up, and choose the in-case power adapter after testing 5 V.
  • The panel's hub only works with a host on its USB-C. HDMI alone leaves the USB-A ports dead. Always run the USB-A→C cable to the PC or the Pi.
  • Miracast has no touch path. Only the Pi routes give you touch wirelessly.
  • Two HDMI sources (GPU and Pi) share one HDMI input. Choose per session, or add a $15 HDMI switch back if you want both cabled at once.
  • Touch on the wrong screen after re-plugging: Tablet PC Settings → Setup.
  • LibreHardwareMonitor needs admin and binds all interfaces: Task Scheduler with highest privileges plus a firewall rule.
  • Magnets do not carry weight in shear; the carrier plate needs a ledge.
